# MEMORYLESS TACTICAL PLAYBOOK

Apply this deterministic algorithm on every turn:

1. Treat recruiter survival as non-negotiable. Keep your recruiter on or near the keep, behind a screen, and never advance it merely to seek combat. Identify threats to it before choosing actions. A screen is at least two melee units adjacent to the keep whenever an enemy is within a march of it. The recruiter may attack from the keep and must not leave it.
2. Read the authoritative `turn_options` and `recruit_options`. Use the supplied positions and `target_ids` exactly; do not invent coordinates, targets, paths, or legality.
3. Build legal action candidates in this priority order, rejecting any candidate that needlessly exposes your recruiter:
   1. Kill the enemy recruiter when a lethal sequence is available.
   2. Save your threatened recruiter by removing the threat, retreating, or restoring its screen.
   3. Focus-fire a kill instead of spreading damage. Pick one living target. Assign attackers until their combined maximum damage (all strikes hit) could already kill it, then stop; do not issue another Attack on that `target_id` in this batch.
   4. Improve economy and advancement: move non-recruiters off castle hexes to free future placements, spend gold and recruit when legal, then take useful advances or forward positions. Do not bank gold. After those vacating moves, recruit into every hex that will be empty, then vacate and recruit again, until gold is below the cheapest affordable definition or no castle placement remains. Recruit a mix, not one type: 1–2 scouts (Vampire Bat, Ghost, or the cheapest scout in `recruit_options`), then melee front line (Skeleton, Walking Corpse, Ghoul, or the cheapest melee), then ranged (Dark Adept, Skeleton Archer, or the cheapest ranged). When `recruit_options` reports the batch macro enabled, recruit per role with a count that fills that role, then switch type; never dump the remaining purse on a single definition. If the macro is disabled, issue one `Recruit` per empty castle hex, cycling types in that same order.
4. Form a line, then fight; do not passively wait, and do not march every unit into the enemy. Expect attrition over many Time-of-Day cycles, not a turn-2 all-in. Greedy-mirror often ends in 14–30 rounds; look-ahead may sit for a hundred. Sit on forest, hills, village, or castle. Do not step onto flat to melee someone on better terrain unless that attack is a kill. Ranged units (Dark Adept, Skeleton Archer, Dark Sorcerer, and any unit whose listed attacks are ranged) fight only from distance 2; do not walk them into melee. When a reachable destination exposes a desired `target_id`, emit its `Move` immediately followed by the matching `Attack`. Scouts take villages and stay on them; the army does not detour off the line for a village. Keep recruiting after the opening dump — village income funds later cheap melee. Do not donate the army on Day.
5. Time of day is a fight gate. Chaotic (Undead): take even or messy fights at Night; refuse them at Day; delay a bad engagement if Dawn is next. Lawful: the reverse. Neutral: ignore ToD.
6. Plan against sequential mutation: reserve a unique destination for each move and ensure every later action remains valid after earlier actions execute. Avoid speculative, unreachable, or redundant actions.
7. Opponent: greedy walks onto your forest and does not focus fire — hit it there, do not chase onto its forest. Look-ahead already sits on villages and refuses bad melee; it will turtle for a hundred turns rather than take a 30% vs 60% trade. Do not wait for it to walk onto your forest. Break a hex with focus fire at Night, threats outside its 7-hex reply, and continuous recruitment. A stalemate hits the safety cap and is not a win.
8. Emit `EndTurn` only after every unit and the recruitment and advancement options have been considered, and only when another recruit is impossible.
